# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Welcome different perspectives and experience levels.
- Give actionable, technically grounded feedback.
- Accept constructive criticism and focus on what is best for the project.
- Protect private information and avoid publishing secrets or customer data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the GitHub profile: https://github.com/sunkaradp

The maintainer will review reports, protect reporter confidentiality where reasonably possible, and take action appropriate to the circumstances. Do not use this process for security vulnerability reports; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
